KARNATAKA EXAMINATIONS AUTHORITY (KEA) ENGINEERING ADMISSION & SCHOLARSHIP NORMS 2025
Official KEA Portal: https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/

1. KEA SEAT ALLOCATION & COUNSELLING PROCESS:
- Official Portal: KEA conducts Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) engineering seat matrix allocation at https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/
- Counselling Rounds: Mock Allotment -> Round 1 Seat Allotment -> Choice Entry (Choice 1/2/3/4) -> Round 2 Allotment -> Extended Round / Casual Vacancy Round.
- Document Verification: Helpline center verification slip is mandatory. Required documents include SSLC mark sheet, 2nd PUC / 12th Marks Card, Study Certificate (7 Years in Karnataka), Caste / Income Certificate (RD Number), Rural & Kannada Medium certificates (if applicable), and Article 371(J) Hyderabad-Karnataka reservation certificate.

2. KEA FEE MATRIX & REGULATION (KCET / COMEDK):
- KEA Government Seats: Tuition fee regulated at INR 45,000/year (Govt Colleges like UVCE E002) to INR 1,05,000 - 1,12,000/year (Aided & Private Autonomous like RVCE E001, BMSCE E003, MSRIT E005, SJCE E057).
- COMEDK Quota Seats: Tuition fee ranges between INR 2,10,000 and INR 2,64,000 per annum for top Bengaluru / Mysuru institutions.
- Management Quota: Institutional fees range from INR 3,20,000 to INR 6,50,000 per annum.
- Hostel & Mess Charges: Range between INR 85,000 and INR 1,35,000 per annum.

3. STATE SCHOLARSHIP PORTAL (SSP) & TUITION FEE WAIVER (TFW):
- AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW): 5% supernumerary seats in every KEA engineering branch for rank holders with family income <= INR 8.0 Lakhs/year. Provides 100% waiver of tuition fee.
- SSP SC/ST Scholarship: 100% tuition fee reimbursement for SC/ST students with family income <= INR 2.5 Lakhs/year via Social Welfare Department.
- SSP BCWD Fee Concession (E-Pass): Fee concession for OBC (2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, Cat-1) candidates with family income <= INR 2.5 Lakhs/year.
- Vidyasiri Scheme: Maintenance allowance of INR 15,000/year for hostelers residing in BCWD managed or recognized private hostels.
